### About
I'm a research engineer working on robot learning, robotics perception, and machine vision systems. I'm especially interested in end-to-end learning and in machine perception problems more broadly.

I'm currently at Boston University, advised by Dr. Wenchao Li of the Dependable Computing Lab and co-advised by Dr. Eshed Ohn-Bar of the H2X group. Most of my work is in simulation: training and evaluating end-to-end driving policies in CARLA, with reinforcement learning in Stable-Baselines3 and a focus on how much a policy's perception limits what it can learn. Earlier in the lab I built perception and manipulation pipelines for a 7-DoF industrial arm, covering camera calibration, homography-based localization, multi-view 3D reconstruction, and a benchmark for vision-language model planning.

I also ship production vision systems. I built [Crown Barber Studio](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crown-barber-studio/id6782692009), a 3D head-scanning iOS app with its own real-time vision and graphics pipeline on ARKit, as Principal Software Engineer.

Before this: BU dual degrees in Physics and Computer Science, with a research background in computational imaging, optics and photonics, and condensed matter. 

Along the way I've been an ML intern at Scale AI, a data science intern at Leuko Labs, and co-founded and serves as the President of the Boston University Artificial Intelligence Society.

Always happy to talk end-to-end learning, machine perception, driving simulation, or 3D reconstruction!
